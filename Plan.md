Plan for Saas AI-Powered customer Support automation and AI-assisted Human communication, this saas Product is AI customer support Platform
with Business Rule Enforcement.

The key differentiator isn't simply that an LLM answers customer questions. It's that every response, whether generated by AI or written by a human agent, passes through an intelligent layer that ensures it follows the company's rules, communication standards, and brand identity.


#Core Concept: 
-A business integrates the saas plugin into its website or application, a customer opens a live chat and starts a conversation, my platform has two operating modes: 

1) Mode 1 - AI Autopilot: 
The AI handles customer conversations independently.
-Customer sends a message.
-AI understands the customer's intent.
-AI retrieves relevant information from the company's knowledge base.
-AI checks company policies and business rules.
-AI generates an appropriate response.
-Response is sent to the customer.

===> If the AI cannot confidently resolve the issue, it escalates the conversation to a human agent.

2) Mode 2 - Human Agent Copilot:
A human agent handles the conversation, but your AI acts as a communication and compliance assistant.
-Customer sends a message.
-Human agent writes a response.
-Before sending, your system intercepts the draft.
-AI checks grammar, spelling, tone, and business rules.
-AI generates a corrected version.
-The agent reviews the suggested version.
-Agent approves, edits, or rejects it.
-Only then is the approved message sent.

#Interesting feature: AI message Verification:

Consider this example.

A company has the following business rules:
- Never promise refunds without checking eligibility.
- Never guarantee delivery dates.
- Always maintain a professional and friendly tone.
- Never disclose internal company information.
- Follow the official refund and cancellation policies.

AGENT'S ORIGINAL MESSAGE

"Don't worry, we will definitely refund you tomorrow. Sorry for the inconvenience, your package should arrive soon."

AI verification results : 

Grammar and spelling
Passed

Business policy compliance
Failed

Brand tone
Needs improvement

Then AI give suggestion version before sending the message with edit and approve option as following the example : 
AI-SUGGESTED VERSION

"We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. We would be happy to review your refund request and check its eligibility. Regarding your package, we'll provide you with the latest available delivery information."

**the AI doesn't just correct grammar. It identifies potentially problematic promises and rewrites the response without changing the agent's underlying intention.

That's a much more valuable proposition than a simple grammar checker.**
